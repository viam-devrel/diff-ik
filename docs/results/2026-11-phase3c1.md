# Diff-IK Phase 3c stage 1 results: the real SO-101, 2026-11-15

**Target:** the real SO-101 follower arm (`follower-arm` on `laptop-main`, so-101 driver v0.14.0, STS3215 servos), driven by the diff-ik CLI and then the `servo` service.
- Task: pointing; `singular_eps` 0.03; `max_damping` 0.05; 50 Hz.
- Start pose `q_start` = (0, −35.0, 26.4, 79.6, 0)°.
- Floor guard 30 mm above the table.

The safety design, the pre-servo checks and the per-run logs are in `dev/so101-hw.md`. The spec is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-diff-ik-phase3c1-design.md`.

**Verdict: stage 1 passes at all three speed levels.** It needed two hardware-side fixes and two measurement fixes, all described below.

## Bus and lag

- **Bus probe at 50 Hz:**
  - joint reads: p50 7.5 ms, p99 14.1 ms;
  - paced read+send: p99 12.1 ms (2 overruns in 500);
  - driver log: `streamed 499 points … late 0`.
- **Loop ticks during servo runs:** p99 2.3–8.2 ms. 50 Hz holds with margin, so the brief's Feetech bus-rate risk doesn't materialise at 5 joints on this Mac.
- **Streaming lag** (cross-correlation of commanded and measured joints, circle runs): **4–6 ticks, 80–120 ms**, against 2 ticks (40 ms) in both sims. Shoulder lift and wrist flex lag 4 ticks; pan and elbow 5–6.
  - The circle's commanded − measured gap peaks at 2.4° (L1), 3.5° (L2) and 3.6° (L3).
  - This is servo-side tracking lag. The driver reports no late points, and the loop never overran.

## Findings and fixes

1. **The pan and wrist-roll servos hunted while holding** (fixed on the servos; no code change).
   - j0 swung 10 counts peak to peak (0.88°) and j4 11, at every pose, even with viam-server stopped.
   - These two joints carry no gravity load, and their registers matched the driver's tuned table (pan P32/D48/I0, roll P48/D48/I0, deadband 1). Raising D (48 → 64) and lowering the minimum startup force (16 → 8) changed nothing.
   - **Deadband 3** stopped it: 0–1 counts after repeated step tests. Deadband 2 still hunted after a move.
   - Applied to servos 1 and 5 with viam-server stopped. The driver never writes deadband registers, so the change persists and needs no config change.
   - Cost: a settle offset of up to 3 counts (0.26°, about 0.8 mm at this reach).
   - Before the fix, `validate` stopped at config 32, because `safeArm` never saw the arm still. That was the fail-safe working as designed.
   - **Driver suggestion:** ship deadband 3 for the unloaded joints alongside the gain table.
2. **The resync rule assumed sim lag** (spec revised).
   - The original rule, max(3 ticks at the level speed, 2 × droop), gave 1.8° at L1. The L1 circle then resynced 23 times (mean 21.9 mm, max 46.0 mm).
   - The new rule, **(measured lag + 3) ticks at the level speed**, gave 0 resyncs at every level: L1 4.8°, L2 9.6°, L3 16.2°.
   - Trade-off: after an obstruction clears, a gap of up to `resync_deg` closes at full servo speed. At L3 that's up to 16°. For stage 2, consider a joint-gap limit in the loop rather than a larger resync.
3. **The singular oscillation bar was below encoder resolution** (measurement fix).
   - `minStep` = 0.1 × limit × dt is 0.06° at L1, under one count (0.088°). Pan wobble inside the deadband counted as 7 oscillations.
   - The bar now applies to the **commanded** joints; measured reversals are reported. On the real arm the commanded joints show at most 1 reversal at every level.
4. **The bench's motion threshold was below encoder resolution** (measurement fix). Its 0.05° threshold gave a false 4 ms latency, and the arm never read as stationary. The new `--motion-eps-deg` (0.2 on hardware, just over 2 counts) fixes both.
5. **Mechanical observations** (not fixed):
   - The elbow tracks slow commands with stick-slip, holding then jumping 0.1–0.5°.
   - After the L2 dead-man ramp, the arm crept until 1102 ms (558 ms at L3).
   - Neither affects any bar.
6. **Driver behaviour confirmed:**
   - Streamed points run uncapped, so the loop's clamp is the only speed limit. The loop clamp held: peak joint speeds stayed within 1.1 × the level limit.
   - The graceful drain makes the driver's `late` line log on every stream; every one read during L1–L3 reports late 0.
   - `Stop` during a profiled move was never exercised. `safeArm` kept it away from profiled moves, and its tests cover that.

## Addendum: streaming smoothness experiments (2026-11-15)

**Read latency.**
- Response delay (register 7) is already 0 on servos 1–5.
- On the bus, five individual reads take 5.3 ms at p50 and one sync read takes 1.3 ms. The driver already uses one sync read (`ServoGroup.Positions`).
- So most of `JointPositions`' 7.5 ms is module or gRPC overhead, not bus time. Not yet profiled.

**Speed-profiled streaming** (so-101 branch `stream-speed-profile`, 790174c, local build).
- Each streamed point gets per-joint goal speed = margin × |Δq| / Δt instead of 0/0 (max speed, unlimited acceleration).
- Off by default, through the `stream_speed_margin`, `stream_min_speed_deg_s` and `stream_accel_deg_s2` attributes.
- Results on the L2 CLI circle at 50 mm/s, middle third of the run:

| Profile | Mean error | Lag j0/j1/j2/j3 (ticks) | Roughness j0/j1/j2/j3 (RMS 2nd difference, °) | Zero-motion ticks j0/j1/j2/j3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0/0 (v0.14.0) | 25.6 mm | 6/5/6/5 | 0.241/0.154/0.174/0.463 | 16/10/17/25 % |
| margin 1.3, floor 10°/s | 24.9 mm | **5/4/5/4** | 0.249/0.156/**0.157/0.301** | 16/10/21/34 % |
| + acceleration 3000°/s² | 25.3 mm | 6/5/6/4 | 0.244/0.147/0.166/0.372 | 17/9/17/27 % |

- The speed-only profile cuts one tick of lag on every joint and a third of the wrist-flex roughness. Elbow stick-slip in `singular` is unchanged (10 measured reversals).
- The acceleration limit gives that back.
- Roughness and zero-motion ticks are limited by the encoder: one count is 0.088°, and at 50 mm/s many joints move under 1–2 counts per tick. The remaining unevenness is mostly the servos' own position loop and friction, not the write profile.
- Left on `laptop-main`: margin 1.3, floor 10°/s.

**Point lead time** (`armIO`, `--lead-ms`, with the speed-profiled driver; L2 circle at 50 mm/s; lag from sub-tick cross-correlation):

| Lead | Lag j0/j1/j2/j3 | Mean error | Driver late |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 ms (one tick, the default) | 97/77/103/75 ms | 24.9 mm | 0 |
| 10 ms | 86/68/93/60 ms | 24.4 mm | 0 |

A 5 ms lead takes about 15 ms off every joint, with no late points (probe and circle, 1226 points per lead).
