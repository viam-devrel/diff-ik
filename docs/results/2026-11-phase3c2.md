# Diff-IK Phase 3c stage 2 results: leader-arm teleop on the real SO-101, 2026-11-15

- **Setup:** `clients/python/teleop.py` streams the SO-101 leader's end-effector pose to the follower through the diff-ik `servo` service, using the **6-DOF pose task**:
  - first an engage session at half the L2 limits;
  - then a tracking session;
  - hold with hysteresis, the floor clamp, the watchdog, and a gripper mirror at 15 Hz.
- **Machine:** `laptop-main` (local plaintext listener, 50 Hz, the default 5 ms lead).
- **Driver:** the follower runs a local so-101 build (`teleop-smooth` = `stream-speed-profile` + `servo-deadband`) with `stream_speed_margin` 1.3. The pan and wrist roll have deadband 3.
- Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-diff-ik-phase3c2-design.md`. Runbook: `dev/so101-hw.md`, stage 2.

**Verdict:** teleop passes the brief's bars. There's no servo-bus saturation in any run (late 0, overruns 0, tick p99 at most 6.6 ms). There's no visible jitter with the `smooth` gain profile. With `tuned`, there's slight visible jitter, which this report traces below.
- The **pose task works on the 5-DOF arm** for targets taken from an identical arm: 100 % rest convergence.
- To make teleop usable, the speed cap and the floor were set for it: 150 mm/s linear, and a floor 5 mm above the table.

## Findings

1. **The linear speed cap dominated lag at L2.**
   - The operator's natural leader speed is 60–150 mm/s, with bursts up to 550 mm/s. At L2's 70 mm/s cap, the follower ran at the cap 41 % of the time, so the measured lag (460 ms) was the cap, not the system.
   - At 150 mm/s (with L3 joint and angular limits), lag fell to 110 ms, close to stage 1's 60–100 ms servo lag.
   - **Teleop needs a linear cap at about the operator's p90 hand speed;** 150 mm/s worked here.
2. **The stage-1 floor (30 mm + 15 mm margin) blocks table picks.**
   - With the leader's jaw on the table, the follower's target was clamped 45 mm up, on 35 % of ticks in run 1.
   - `--min-z-mm -10` puts the follower's jaw tip at least 5 mm above the table. The follower reached 4.1 mm (tuned) and 2.8 mm (smooth, which droops). That's 1 % clamping, and the watchdog never tripped.
   - Smooth's droop uses up most of that 5 mm. For `smooth`, add the droop to the margin.
3. **The pose task on 5 DOF works for identical-arm targets:** 100 % rest convergence in the smoke run and in run 2, at 0.5–0.8 mm and 0.2–0.3°. The wrist roll follows the leader.
4. **Where the `tuned` jitter comes from.**
   - 14 of the 20 follower steps over 1 mm while the leader was held came within 0.6 s of the hold starting. That's the follower finishing its catch-up after the leader stops, roughly the 110 ms lag plus settling.
   - The rest are 1–2 mm steps mid-hold, consistent with stick-slip and backlash at the stiffer gains. The user observed significant backlash on the loaded joints.
   - `smooth` cuts follower motion while held by about 4× (p95 0.37 vs 1.38 mm), and the user saw no jitter.
5. **`smooth` vs `tuned` for teleop.** `smooth` looks better to the operator, but costs 2.7× the lag (295 vs 110 ms), about 60 % more tracking error during motion, and about 6 mm / 2° of sag at rest.
   - **Teleop, where the operator closes the loop by eye:** prefer `smooth`.
   - **Precise servoing** (stage 3, AprilTag): `tuned`.

   This is the use split the driver's `servo_gain_profile` docs already describe.
6. **Engage with a hand-held leader.** The original engage condition ("converged for 0.5 s") failed once: the operator moved the leader during engage, because there's no visible cue that engage has finished.
   - Engage now ends once the follower is within 10 mm / 5° of the leader (or within the session tolerance, if larger) for 0.2 s, with a 30 s timeout.
   - The tracking session closes the rest of the gap.
7. **Calibration between the two arms.**
   - Their calibrated ranges differ: the leader's elbow reaches 95.6° against the follower's 84.8°, and its shoulder lift −105.8° against −98.5°. A folded leader can be outside the follower's range.
   - Posed to match by eye, the leader read +15° on pan and −15° on wrist flex relative to the follower. That's either posing error or calibration offsets.
   - Teleop ran fine as is, because the operator closes the loop visually. For absolute 1:1 accuracy, recalibrate the leader, or have the client measure a joint offset at engage.
8. **Client robustness, verified in the sim rehearsal:** SIGINT and SIGTERM mid-run, Ctrl-C at a prompt, and an injected fault all exit cleanly. The exit stops the session, re-torques the leader and frees its trigger, with a 3 s timeout per step. A hung per-tick RPC becomes an error after 0.5 s.
   - The rehearsal also showed that a non-interactive shell starts background jobs with SIGINT ignored. Signal tests need a harness that restores SIGINT.

## Recommended teleop settings (SO-101, this setup)

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Rate / lead | 50 Hz / 5 ms (default) |
| Task | pose (6-DOF) |
| Limits | joints 90°/s, linear **150 mm/s**, angular 45°/s, `resync_deg` 16.2 |
| Floor | `--min-z-mm -10` (jaw tip ≥ 5 mm above the table); more margin with `smooth` |
| Gain profile | `smooth` for operator-driven teleop (with `--tol-mm 13 --tol-deg 5`); `tuned` for precise servoing |
| Engage | at half the limits, ends within 10 mm / 5° (or the session tolerance) for 0.2 s |
